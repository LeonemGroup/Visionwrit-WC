The method

Three simple steps

You don't have, because you don't ask. So ask, and keep asking.

1

Write it once

List everything you hope for. Big, bold, and specific, with a photo for each ask.

2

Read it daily

Every morning when you wake and every night before bed. Visionwrit reminds you.

3

Look back

In 6 to 12 months, count what you've been given and share your testimony.

The Make it plain meter

Write it out completely, so you'll know it when it comes.

Every time you write an ask, the Make it plain meter checks it for four things and fills in as your ask gets clearer. If something is missing, it tells you what to add.

  1. What it isSay exactly what you're asking for.
  2. DetailsHow it looks, how big, what kind.
  3. Name or numberA name, brand, year, or amount.
  4. When or whereA date, a season, or a place.

The meter only encourages. It never stops you from saving an ask.
