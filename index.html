The method

Three simple steps

You don't have, because you don't ask. So ask, and keep asking.

1

Write it once

List everything you hope for. Big, bold, and specific, with a photo for each ask.

2

Read it daily

Every morning when you wake and every night before bed. Visionwrit reminds you.

3

Look back

In 6 to 12 months, count what you've been given and share your testimony.

Make it plain

The clearer you write it, the easier it is to recognize when it comes.

As you write, the Make it plain meter suggests details to add: what it is, how it looks, a name or number, and when or where. It only encourages. It never stops you from saving.

Inside the app

Everything you need to keep your vision in front of you

Asks with photos

Write each ask, add a picture, and sort with tags like Career, Home, Family and Travel.

Morning and evening reading

A full-screen reading mode, one ask at a time, with reminders at the times you choose.

Vision board

See your asks as a board, play a slideshow, and save it as a phone wallpaper or share image.

Received and testimonies

Mark an ask received and write down the story of how it came, so you never forget.

Look back

Every 6 or 12 months, go through your asks: received, on its way, or still asking.

Private by design

No sign-up needed to start. Your asks stay on your phone, with an optional app lock.
