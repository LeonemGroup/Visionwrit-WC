Getting started

What is Visionwrit?+

Visionwrit is a vision board and asks journal for your phone. You write down what you hope for as clear, specific asks with a photo, read them every morning and night, and look back to count what you've received. It's built on Habakkuk 2:2: write the vision, and make it plain.

What is an "ask"?+

An ask is one thing you're hoping for, written plainly. For example, instead of "a better job," you might write "A full-time remote job I love, paying $85,000 a year, by June 2027." Each ask can have a photo, a short title for your board, and tags.

Do I need an account?+

No. You can start writing right away, and everything works without internet. After your third ask, Visionwrit offers an optional account so you can back up your asks.

Is Visionwrit available on iPhone?+

Not yet. Visionwrit launches on Android first, with an iPhone version planned for 2027. You can vote for it on the Feature Requests page.

Do I have to be a person of faith to use it?+

No. Faith is part of Visionwrit's foundation, but it's never forced. Today's word mixes Bible verses with gratitude and wisdom quotes, and you can turn Scripture off in Settings.

Reading & reminders

How does reading mode work?+

Tap "Start reading" on the Home screen. Visionwrit shows your asks full screen, one at a time. Morning reading switches to evening reading in the afternoon to match the greeting.

How do I change my reminder times?+

In Settings, under Reading reminders, tap a time to change it. The free version includes one reminder (morning or evening). Plus includes both.

What is Today's word?+

A short verse or quote on your Home screen each day. Bible verses come from the World English Bible, alongside public domain gratitude and wisdom quotes. Choose what you see in Settings, under Daily word.

Why does the app change color in the evening?+

Visionwrit uses Morning light from 6 AM to 6 PM and Night sky the rest of the time. To keep one look, go to Settings, then Appearance, and choose Always morning light or Always night sky.

Board & sharing

How do I make a phone wallpaper from my board?+

Open the Board tab and tap the download button. Choose the phone wallpaper size and up to 10 photos, then save or share the image.

Can I filter my board?+

Yes. Tap a tag at the top of the Board, Asks list, or reading mode to show only those asks.

What does "Share my count" share?+

Only your numbers, such as how many asks you've received. It never shares the asks themselves.

Visionwrit Plus

What's the difference between Free and Plus?+

Free includes up to 20 asks, one reading reminder, Today's word, the vision board, and a look back every 12 months. Plus adds unlimited asks, morning and evening reminders, sharing without the Visionwrit footer, and a look back every 6 months. Cloud backup and premium board themes are coming to Plus soon.

How much does Plus cost?+

$19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, $3.99 a month, or $49.99 once for lifetime access. Prices may vary by country and are shown in Google Play before you buy.

How do I cancel?+

Open the Play Store, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and choose Visionwrit. Plus stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

What happens to my asks if Plus ends?+

Nothing is deleted. Every ask stays readable. You just can't add more than 20 until you renew.

I bought Plus but the app doesn't show it.+

In Settings, open Visionwrit Plus and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Google account you used to buy.

Privacy & data

Who can see my asks?+

Only you. Your asks are stored on your phone.

Can I lock the app?+

Yes. In Settings, under Privacy and data, turn on Lock Visionwrit. Visionwrit also hides its screen in the app switcher while locked.

Can I export my asks?+

Yes. In Settings, under Privacy and data, choose Export my asks (PDF). It includes every ask with its photo, ready to save or print.

How do I erase everything?+

In Settings, under Privacy and data, choose Erase all Visionwrit data. This permanently deletes your asks and photos from the app and can't be undone. To remove a single ask, Archive keeps it out of your reading while Delete removes it for good.

Troubleshooting

I'm not getting reminders.+

Check that notifications are allowed for Visionwrit in your phone's Settings, and that battery saver isn't restricting the app. Then open Visionwrit's Settings and confirm your reminder is turned on.

I can't add a photo to an ask.+

Visionwrit needs permission to use your photos or camera. Go to your phone's Settings, then Apps, then Visionwrit, then Permissions, and allow Photos and Camera.

How do I move my asks to a new phone?+

Your asks live on your phone. Before switching, use Export my asks (PDF) in Settings to keep a copy. Cloud backup and sync for Plus members is coming soon and will move them automatically.
